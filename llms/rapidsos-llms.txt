# RapidSOS

> RapidSOS operates what it calls "the world's largest Safety Network," connecting public safety agencies (911/ECCs), enterprises, and connected devices to deliver AI-powered emergency detection, verification, and escalation for faster emergency response. Its APIs let technology partners send emergency-related information into the RapidSOS safety platform and let public-safety partners consume that data.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public RapidSOS developer surface. RapidSOS does not publish an open OpenAPI specification; the API documentation is gated behind a partner login at developer.rapidsos.com.

## API Products
- [Alerts](https://developer.rapidsos.com/): Streamline the flow of critical safety information to emergency services for faster, informed responses.
- [IAR (IamResponding)](https://developer.rapidsos.com/): Emergency response platform providing critical incident data to first responders.
- [Public Safety Partners](https://developer.rapidsos.com/): For integrators displaying emergency-related information from the safety platform.
- [Tech Partners](https://developer.rapidsos.com/): Send emergency-related information to the RapidSOS safety platform via the partner APIs.
